About CoursePath

Problem

Students can find it difficult to understand which subjects should be completed before advanced courses.

Solution

CoursePath represents prerequisites as a directed graph and combines it with an academic planner.

Technology

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, SVG, LocalStorage, Node.js, Express and MySQL.

Project Objectives

  1. Visualize course dependencies.
  2. Help students choose courses logically.
  3. Provide a semester planning interface.
  4. Demonstrate database integration.
  5. Provide a responsive and user-friendly interface.
